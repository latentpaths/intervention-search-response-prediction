# Published Boolean models

These `.bnet` files come from `Benchmarks/models` in
[boolmore](https://github.com/kyuhyongpark/boolmore) at commit
`a757a6d13c9302f3b8dcdc20db85ab90681f42f8`, associated with
[Park, Rozum, and Albert (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41540-025-00532-y).
The source directory has 74 `.bnet` files, 40 of them with at most 30 nodes.
The 39 files here have 9--30 nodes; the sole smaller model is named in
`manifest.json` with its exclusion reason. That manifest also records a SHA-256
hash for each unmodified included file. `LICENSE.boolmore` is the source
repository's MIT license. The published models are executable abstractions,
not measurements from new biological experiments.
